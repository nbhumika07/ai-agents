# AI-Personalised-Study-Planner


A Python-based personalised study timetable generator that creates a feasible study plan based on a student's exam deadline, topic difficulty, preparation level, available study time, and most productive study hours.

## Project Overview

Students often struggle to divide their limited study time effectively across multiple topics. This project addresses that problem by generating a personalised study timetable based on the student's individual study constraints and topic requirements.

The system gives higher priority to topics that are:

- More difficult
- Less prepared
- More demanding in terms of learning effort

It also changes the study approach as the exam approaches, moving from learning to practice and finally to revision.
